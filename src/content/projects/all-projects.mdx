# Apsona for Salesforce

## 📌 Overview
Apsona for Salesforce is a productivity and automation platform built on top of Salesforce, designed to extend CRM capabilities with reporting, document automation, and workflow tools.  
It allows Salesforce users to generate complex reports, automate document merges, and integrate external services without leaving the Salesforce environment.  

One critical part of Apsona’s ecosystem is the **payment and subscription system**, which ensures that features and services are available only to licensed customer orgs.  
This system validates entitlements, manages client-specific usage, and integrates with Salesforce orgs for secure feature access.

### [BlindShare – P2P Secure Sharing Suite](/projects/personal/blindshare/)
**Sep 2026**

A **peer-to-peer sharing suite** built on WebRTC — every byte travels directly between two browsers, DTLS-encrypted by the browser itself. The signaling server sees only SDP/ICE handshake metadata and holds nothing else.

- Six purpose-built tools: **BlindDrop** (files), **BlindText** (messages), **BlindSnippet** (code with syntax highlighting), **BlindSecret** (credential-safe masked field), **BlindShow** (view-once media), **BlindCast** (live P2P screen share).
- Files chunked as 16–64 KB `ArrayBuffer` slices over `RTCDataChannel`, reassembled as a `Blob` on the receiver — no upload, no cloud storage.
- **Zero-knowledge signaling server** — in-memory room state only during handshake, no payloads, no logs, no database.
- **Tech:** React, Vite, TypeScript, Node.js, WebRTC (`RTCDataChannel`, `MediaStream`), WebSocket signaling.
- **Deployed:** Frontend on Cloudflare (blindshare.in) · Signaling on Faable.

### [BlindParty – P2P Watch Party & Video Call](/projects/personal/blindparty/)
**Sep 2026**

A **P2P watch party and video calling app** for small groups — camera, mic, screen sharing, and ephemeral group chat, all flowing directly between browsers over a full WebRTC mesh.

- **Watch party:** share your screen, tab, or window; all participants watch P2P in real time. (DRM-protected content — Netflix, Prime, Hotstar — is blocked by HDCP/output-protection; works with local video, YouTube, games, slides.)
- **Video call:** camera + mic grid for all participants, per-peer mute/camera toggles, connection-state indicators.
- **Group chat:** ephemeral text fanned out peer-to-peer over each participant's `RTCDataChannel` — never touches the server.
- **Renegotiation-capable:** camera, mic, and screen share can be toggled mid-call without dropping connections (`useGroupWebRTC` hook manages one `RTCPeerConnection` per peer).
- Max room size: 6 peers (full mesh; SFU like mediasoup/LiveKit needed beyond that).
- **Tech:** React, Vite, TypeScript, Node.js, WebRTC (`RTCPeerConnection` per peer, `getDisplayMedia`), WebSocket signaling.
- **Deployed:** Frontend on Cloudflare (party.blindshare.in) · Signaling on Faable.
